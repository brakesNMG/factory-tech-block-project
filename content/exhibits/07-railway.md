# Locomotive
year: 1829
by: George & Robert Stephenson
summary: Rocket and the Liverpool & Manchester Railway made fast steam travel real.

## Page: Moving goods and people
Raw cotton arrived at Liverpool; finished cloth was sold worldwide. Canals and horse-drawn wagons were slow. Mill owners wanted a faster link.

## Page: Rocket (1829)
At the Rainhill Trials in 1829, the Stephensons' "Rocket" won the contest for the best locomotive. It used a multi-tube boiler to make steam quickly and reached around 30 mph.

## Page: Liverpool & Manchester Railway (1830)
The world's first inter-city railway with timetabled steam trains opened in 1830, carrying both goods and passengers.

Soon rail lines spread across Britain and the world.

## Page: Who paid the price?
The railway opened to cheers, but the day ended in tragedy. William Huskisson, a Member of Parliament, was struck by the Rocket at the opening and died that day.

Building railways was hard, dangerous labour done by thousands of workers known as navvies.
